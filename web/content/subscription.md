Pick the beans and how often you want them. We roast on the schedule and you stop thinking about it.

## The deal

- **15% off** every bag, for as long as the subscription runs.
- **Free delivery**, on every shipment, with no minimum.
- A cadence of **2, 4 or 6 weeks**.
- First shipment goes out on the next roast day after you subscribe.

## Changing it

Anything can change before the next roast date: pause a shipment, skip one, swap to a different coffee, change the grind, or move the cadence. Changes after the roast date apply to the shipment after that.

## Cancelling

Cancel any time, from the account page or by asking. There is no minimum term, no notice period, and no fee. A cancellation takes effect immediately unless that week's coffee is already roasted.

## Gifting

A subscription can be sent to a different address with a note. It renews to the giver's card until they cancel it.
