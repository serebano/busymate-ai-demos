Where we ship

The United States and Canada. Canadian orders clear customs as roasted coffee with no duty under CUSMA; allow one extra day at the border during December.

Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number by email the moment it is handed to the carrier. Signed-in customers can also just ask the assistant "where is my order" and it will read the current status, the carrier and the expected date straight from the order book.

If it goes missing

If tracking has not moved for three business days, tell us and we re-roast and re-send the order the same day — there is nothing to prove and nothing to fill in.
