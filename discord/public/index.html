Before you write in

A few things players ask first

Can I get a refund?
Within 14 days of buying, if the game has been played for less than two hours. Outside that, ask anyway — a person at the studio still reads it.
Is Sundog Rally finished?
No, and the price says so. Eleven of sixteen stages are in, the replay editor is not, and old replays stopped loading in 0.9.4 when the format changed.
Do your games run on a handheld PC?
Lumen Drift and Ironroot Tactics do, with handheld-sized text since 2.2.7. Sundog Rally does not yet.
I bought a key from a reseller — is it supported?
Yes. Redeem it on the studio's key service and the refund window runs from the redemption date instead.
Will you tell me what you are working on next?
Forge will not — the studio announces a new game when there is a build to show, not before.
