not sourced from any real business. Every catalogue item on the page is

than a stock photo, since almost every real product photo of consumer
electronics on stock-photo sites visibly shows a real manufacturer's brand —
deliberately avoided here. Testimonial avatars are plain initials on a
colour chip (CSS), not photos, since the reviewers are invented.
