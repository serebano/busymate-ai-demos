What the handbook says

  • 27 days plus 8 public holidays, accruing 2.25 days a month.
  • Up to 5 days carry over, and lapse on 31 March.
  • An approver answers within 2 working days; over 10 days in a row needs 4 weeks' notice.
  • Sickness is self-certified for 7 days; a fit note from day 8 goes to the people team, not your team.
  • 2 volunteering days and 5 study days a year, on top of annual leave.

Two anchor days

Tuesday and Thursday in Bristol or Leeds; client work always comes first.

20 days abroad

Work from another country for up to 20 working days a year, with three weeks' notice for the tax check.

Parental leave

26 weeks at full pay for the primary carer, 8 for the second, and a phased four-day first month back.

The dates that decide when money moves

Salaries land on the 26th, or the last working day before it. Anything that changes a payslip — an expense claim, a benefit choice, a new bank account — has to be with payroll by the 12th to make that run. Bram works these out live rather than reading a fixed list.

Asked most weeks

The five that fill the people team's inbox

How many days carry into next year?
Up to 5, and they must be taken by 31 March or they lapse. Ask Bram and it will tell you what is left on yours once you are signed in.
When can I change my benefits?
Open enrolment is 1–21 October, effective 1 November. New joiners have 30 days, and a life event — a birth, a marriage, a partner losing cover — allows a change within 60 days.
I lost a receipt. Can I still claim?
Under £10, yes. Over that, tell the people team what happened rather than guessing — it is usually fine, and the £30 overnight meal allowance never needs receipts.
Can I work from another country for a month?
Up to 20 working days a year, in a country on the approved list, with three weeks' notice so tax and insurance can be checked. Longer than that needs a conversation.
Something personal has come up. Who do I talk to?
A person, every time. Ask Bram for a people partner and the conversation moves to a named one the same day, in confidence.
