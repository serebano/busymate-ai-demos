What the demo shows

Your own mailbox, threaded

The agency connects the inbox customers already write to through a hosted sign-in. Replies go out from that address with the standard mail headers, so every answer lands in the same thread.

A calendar invitation, by mail

When a call is the right next step, the assistant proposes one slot and sends a standard calendar invitation the traveller accepts from their own calendar.

The operator's copilot

In the Inbox the same thread carries AI draft, Summarize, Translate, Forward, Invite and Send. Drafts are never sent without a confirmation.

Grounded, with tools

An MCP server over the itineraries and bookings, WebMCP booking actions on this page, and a signed-in traveller's own booking lookup — the same on the widget and by mail.
