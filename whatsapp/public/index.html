What changes once the number is real

The same brain, knowledge and tools above — just reached the way Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform actually works.

1

A guest messages first

A "Message us" link, a QR code on the table, or a saved contact opens WhatsApp with Marlow's Kitchen's number pre-filled — the guest always starts the conversation, never the business.

2

Meta's Cloud API delivers it

The message arrives over Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API webhook, gets normalized into the same conversation shape as the web chat, and Marlow answers from the same menu and policies shown above.

3

The 24-hour window, then templates

Marlow can reply freely for 24 hours after the guest's last message. After that, only a pre-approved template message (a booking reminder, say) can restart the thread — standard WhatsApp Business rules, same as any provider.

Before you message us

A few things people ask first

Do I need a reservation, or can I just walk in?
Bar and terrace seats are first-come. The dining room is best booked ahead — easiest by messaging Marlow.
Is there parking?
Street parking, plus a public lot two blocks north on Elm.
Can the kitchen work around an allergy?
Tell Marlow when you book — the kitchen can flag or adjust most dishes for common allergens.
What about a party of 9 or more?
Message us for the private-dining pack — Marlow can point larger groups the right way, and a person picks it up from there.
Do you sell gift cards?
Yes — digital gift cards sent by text or WhatsApp, no expiry.
