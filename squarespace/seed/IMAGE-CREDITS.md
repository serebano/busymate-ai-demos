# Image credits — Quiet Pines Yoga (Squarespace demo)

Unlike the other demos in this repo (BigCommerce, Webflow, Wix, …), where we hand-pick and
credit individual free-licence photos ourselves, this site's photography was **selected and
placed automatically by Squarespace's own Blueprint AI** during the "Build a Website" onboarding
flow (`squarespace.com/templates/get-started` → AI Website Builder), based on the site topic
("Yoga Studios & Classes") and brand personality ("Professional") we chose.

- **Source:** Squarespace's own Blueprint AI stock/generated-imagery pipeline. Squarespace does
  not expose the underlying licence metadata (photographer, stock house, or "AI-generated")
  per image in the editor or in `?format=json` — there is no per-image credit to extract.
- **Usage rights:** covered by Squarespace's own Terms of Service for content placed by its
  Blueprint AI product on a site built with it (https://www.squarespace.com/terms-of-service).
  We did not license or select any image individually, so we cannot credit an individual
  photographer the way `sites/bigcommerce/seed/IMAGE-CREDITS.md` does.
- **Pages carrying Blueprint AI imagery:** Home (hero: yoga session on a rooftop deck at
  golden hour; secondary: instructor with small group), About (studio interior), Services
  (same rooftop hero, reused), Appointments (no photography — a booking list).

If this demo is ever promoted past "building" status with independently-sourced photography
(matching the other demos' pattern), replace this file with a real per-image credit table and
swap the images in the Squarespace editor first.
