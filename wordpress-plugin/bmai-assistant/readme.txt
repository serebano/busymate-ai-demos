Add your mate — a grounded AI assistant — to your site in minutes. Connect your Busymate AI account, no code required.

== Description ==

Busymate AI puts a real, grounded AI assistant on your WordPress site — one
that answers from your own content, hands off to a person when it should,
and (optionally) reads your WooCommerce catalogue and orders to answer
customer questions.

This plugin does three things:

1. **Loads the assistant** on your site, as a corner button every visitor
   can open — or only on the pages where you place the included block or
   shortcode.
2. **Connects your account** with one click — "Connect your Busymate AI
   account" takes you through your workspace's own sign-in and consent
   screen (standard OAuth 2.0 with PKCE) and comes straight back with your
   workspace filled in. No slug or key to find and paste.
3. **Optionally reads your WooCommerce store** — one more click sends you
   to your OWN store's WooCommerce authorization screen (WooCommerce's own
   built-in mechanism for this), and your mate can then answer "where is my
   order" and "do you have this in blue" questions, read-only. It never
   places an order, changes one, or issues a refund.

= What this plugin does NOT do =

* It does not gate any feature behind a licence key, a quota or a trial —
  everything above works fully for every install. Your Busymate AI
  workspace has its own plan and billing; this plugin has none of its own.
* It does not change how the assistant looks, what it says or what it can
  do — all of that is served live from your connected workspace, so this
  plugin never needs an update when your workspace settings change.
* It does not touch `header.php`, `wp_head`, or any theme file. Every
  script it loads goes through WordPress's own `wp_enqueue_scripts`.

== Installation ==

1. In wp-admin go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose this zip, and
   activate it.
2. Open **Busymate AI** (under WooCommerce, or under Settings if
   WooCommerce is not installed) and click **Connect your Busymate AI
   account**.
3. Sign in and approve the connection. Your workspace address fills in
   automatically.
4. Optional: click **Connect WooCommerce** to let your mate answer product
   and order questions.
5. Optional: add the **Busymate AI trigger** block, or the
   `[bmai_assistant]` shortcode, anywhere you want an inline button that
   opens the assistant — for example with a starting question:
   `[bmai_assistant label="Ask about sizing" prompt="What sizes do you have?"]`

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Do I need a Busymate AI account? =

Yes — this plugin connects an existing workspace. Create one free at
https://busymate.ai before installing.

= Does "Connect your account" share my WordPress login with Busymate AI? =

No. It uses standard OAuth 2.0 with PKCE: your browser is sent to your OWN
Busymate AI sign-in and consent screen, and this site never sees your
Busymate AI password. Nothing is shared going the other direction either —
your WordPress users and their data never leave this site.

= What does the WooCommerce connection share? =

Your store's own WooCommerce REST API key (generated by WooCommerce itself,
scoped to read-only) and, when a visitor asks about their order, that
visitor's own order history matched to their verified email — never your
whole customer list, and nothing is ever written back to your store.

= Can I use the assistant without WooCommerce? =

Yes. WooCommerce only adds the optional product/order reading — the chat
assistant itself works on any WordPress site.

= Where does the assistant load? =

Everywhere by default — a small corner button. Turn "Every page" off in the
plugin's settings to load it only on pages carrying the block or shortcode.

= I don't want to click through the connect screen — can I set it up by hand? =

Yes. The settings screen has an "enter your workspace address manually"
option below the connect button.
