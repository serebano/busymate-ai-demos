Camber Bay, since 2018

It started because nothing survived a season

Two open-water swimmers kept wearing suits out in a single summer — bagged at the seat, faded at the straps, gone by September. So they went looking for a knit that would not: a 210 gsm regenerated nylon with enough elastane to hold its shape through chlorine, salt and sun cream, knitted by a family mill and cut in one small workroom.

Every colourway soaks for ninety hours in chlorinated water before it is signed off. Anything that shifts goes back to the mill. That is the whole quality process, and it is why the range is twelve pieces and not two hundred.

  • Knits — regenerated nylon, spun from reclaimed fishing net and fabric waste
  • Linen — washed European flax, 165 gsm, softer every wash
  • Repairs — a seam that fails inside 12 months is fixed or replaced
How it is made

Small runs, mended not replaced

Pieces are cut in runs of a few hundred, not tens of thousands, so a fit note from a customer in March changes the pattern by June. Nothing is made to be thrown away after a season: send a failed seam back inside twelve months and it comes home mended, or replaced if it cannot be.

Offcuts go to the same mill that knits the nylon. Packaging is paper, printed with the return instructions, because a second bag should never be needed to send something back.

The five that come up first

How long do I have to send something back?
Thirty days from delivery, unworn, tags on, hygiene liner in place. Free label inside the United States; $9 comes off the refund elsewhere.
I'm between two sizes — which one?
On a swim knit take the larger, because the knit relaxes in water; on linen take the smaller. Give Sol your measurements and it will name the size instead of guessing.
Will the colour survive a chlorinated pool?
Every colourway soaks ninety hours in chlorinated water before it is signed off. Rinse cold after a swim and dry flat in the shade and it will outlast the season.
Can I cancel an order I just placed?
Yes, while it is still being picked. Once it has left the workroom it has to come back as a return instead — Sol can tell you which state an order is in.
Do you do wholesale or team orders?
For a club or a shop, ask for a person in the chat and someone from the workroom picks the same conversation up.
